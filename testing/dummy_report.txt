CONFIDENTIAL DOCUMENT - INTERNAL AUDIT REPORT

Date: August 15, 2026
Subject: Investigation of Fund Flows to Shell Entities

Based on the latest internal audit, it was discovered that Mr. Budi Santoso, as the CEO of PT Maju Bersama, gave a suspicious fund transfer instruction.

Funds amounting to Rp 5,000,000,000 (Five Billion Rupiah) were sent from PT Maju Bersama to an account belonging to Global Nusantara Ltd., located in the Cayman Islands.
It was later discovered that Mrs. Siti Aminah, who serves as a Commissioner at PT Maju Bersama, is the ultimate Beneficial Owner of Global Nusantara Ltd.

Furthermore, Budi Santoso is also recorded as owning an asset in the form of a luxury property in the Pondok Indah area, Jakarta, which is suspected to have been purchased using a portion of those funds.
